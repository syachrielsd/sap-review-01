# Screening — One Star, Written in Sap

Mission: one line, 1-star review from a houseplant about its human owner, 1–279
chars. Judge criteria in order: (1) on-topic houseplant review, grievance at the
centre; (2) funnier — real laugh, surprising turn, sharp plant's-eye grievance;
(3) sounds like a houseplant wrote it (water, light, soil, pots, repotting,
being moved); (4) reads as a genuine one-star review, clear complaint + reason
for the single star, one line under 280 chars; (5) fresh, specific detail,
precise wording; NO stock phrases about drama queens, dramatic wilting, or
being ignored. Scores 1–5 per criterion, in order. Total /25.

## Notes

- #4, #12 and #13 tie at 24/25. Tie-break is the landing: #12 ends on a
  single repeated word ("Twelve."), the punch word last, after a setup that
  misdirects (the reader expects the complaint to be the misting, then the
  real grievance is the performance of care: one mist, one post, a self-awarded
  title, and zero water since). #4 lands on "I am done", #13 buries its best
  image (the sourdough) mid-sentence. #12 wins.
- #12's angle is fresh for this field: performative plant parenting — the human
  did the care once for the audience, not for the plant. No banned stock
  phrases anywhere. Concrete plant images: misting, watering, the pot's owner
  calling himself "plant dad". The receipt is the number: 12 likes, repeated.
- Rejected angles kept out of the winner: "dramatic wilting" / "being ignored"
  (banned stock), generic thirst, "he forgot me" without a receipt.

## Winner

**#12 — 24/25, 142 characters:**

> One star. He misted me once, posted about it, and now calls himself a plant dad. I have not been watered since. The post got 12 likes. Twelve.
